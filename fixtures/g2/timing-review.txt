G4 fixed timing input record

The original G2 catalog and byte-snippet review remain in extraction-review.txt.
scenario-timed.json is a separate synthetic scheduling conformance input. It
preserves those sources, patch bytes, modeled check facts, and company claims.
Its timing values are additional exercise assumptions, not measured LAE work.

All original candidate duration-model IDs have explicit definitions and operation
scopes. TM-NAV has zero ticks; other models have one tick. The window is [0,30],
seed is 0, and the per-tick consumption bound is 100. These values demonstrate
scheduler behavior and do not claim productivity or empirical calibration.
T10-REVERT binds the existing G3 patch.revert capability to TM-REVERT and requires
an applied overlay. This adds no new patch, AI collaboration, or G5 behavior.

Optional controls are scheduler conformance inputs: priority20 denotes an external
control and priority40 a decision control. Each control names one candidate and
an explicit cancellation or invalidation effect. Effects apply immediately after
their cause is consumed; generation1 denotes a uniquely identified reservation.
Task resumption and later generations belong to G5 and are not modeled here.

Optional jitter_ticks adds bounded synthetic jitter via raw % (jitter_ticks+1),
with no claim of exact statistical uniformity, using
lae-keyed-sha256-v1. Its key is (operation-duration, actor, candidate, model,
occurrence), and the raw 64-bit value is retained in the reservation event.
The default input has no jitter and uses no draws. Replay verifies the fixed
mapping without consuming any random stream.
